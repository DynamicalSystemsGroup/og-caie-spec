Every quote on this site carries one of three tags. *Machine*: the tests locate the quote in a content-hashed snapshot of the source. *Human*: a named person verified it against the source on a date, usually an ISO screenshot held locally. *Pending*: transcribed and awaiting that person's tick, listed on a rulings sheet; a pending quote is cited but not yet verified.
