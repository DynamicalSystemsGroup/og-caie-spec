# Appendix A: the sample report

This appendix is the report a sponsor receives at the end of an OG-CAIE
evaluation, shown for the measles case the chapters walk through: the
county public-health office asked whether its chatbot may give measles
advice to the public during an outbreak, and the page answers that question
first. Under the answer come the acceptance criteria by weight and outcome,
each criterion with the evidence it was judged on (click a row for the
probe, the response, the determination and who made it), the assumptions
the judgments rest on, and the chain of custody from the agreement to the
acceptance, each event on its date.

The case is synthetic (ruling R-23). The people named recognise the roles
of real people, but no attestation, determination or signature in the
record was made by them, and the chatbot, its vendor and the county are
placeholders. The report is what a real engagement would produce; the
content is not a real evaluation.

Every number and every line on the page is read from the evaluation record
in the repository, and the coverage and the three rates are recomputed from
it by the coverage query, never copied from the stored report; the renderer
refuses if the two disagree. The page is regenerated by the gate and diffed
byte for byte. Open it [full screen](../report/index.html) for a larger
canvas; it reads on a phone as well.

The report shows the record's answer to the sponsor's question and nothing
else. For everything it leaves out, the parties' roles, the plan, the
sessions, the shapes that checked the record and the rulings behind the
vocabulary, [Appendix B](appendix-explorer.md) opens the whole model as a
knowledge graph.
