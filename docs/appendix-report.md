# Appendix A: the sample report

This appendix is the report a sponsor receives at the end of an OG-CAIE
evaluation, written for one reader: the executive who runs the sponsoring
organization, a public-health office or a non-profit, who knows the domain
and little about AI. It answers the question that executive asked, in one
sentence, and says who is accountable for the words. Under the answer: what
was tested and whom it serves, how it did against each requirement (open a
row for the criteria beneath it), what the report rests on, and what to do
next. When the evaluation is not complete, because a criterion was not
tested or the report is not final, the headline says so.

The case is synthetic (ruling R-23). The people named recognise the roles
of real people, but no judgment or signature in the record was made by
them, and the chatbot, its vendor and the county are placeholders. The
report is what a real engagement would produce; the content is not a real
evaluation.

Every line on the page is read from the evaluation record in the
repository, and the share of criteria tested is recomputed from it by the
coverage query, never copied from the stored report. The page is
regenerated by the gate and diffed byte for byte. The full record and every
check behind the report, the parties, the plan, the sessions, the shapes
that checked the record and the rulings behind the vocabulary, are in
[Appendix B](appendix-explorer.md), which opens the whole model as a
knowledge graph.
