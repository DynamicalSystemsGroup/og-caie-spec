# ISA 500 Audit Evidence (IAASB) — digest

Source: `src:isa-500`, held locally. Used as seeAlso for the quality/quantity
reading of appropriateness and sufficiency. PDF p. 2 (definitions, paragraph 5).

- **5(b) Appropriateness (of audit evidence)**: "The measure of the quality of audit evidence; that is, its relevance and its reliability in providing support for the conclusions on which the auditor's opinion is based."
- **5(f) Sufficiency (of audit evidence)**: "The measure of the quantity of audit evidence. The quantity of the audit evidence needed is affected by the auditor's assessment of the risks of material misstatement and also by the quality of such audit evidence."
- **A8**: "The sufficiency and appropriateness of audit evidence are interrelated."
