# ISO/IEC 17000:2020(en) Conformity assessment — Vocabulary — digest

Source: `src:iso-iec-17000-2020`, cite-only (Online Browsing Platform, clause 3
publicly browsable). One term.

- **4.2 object of conformity assessment**: "entity to which specified requirements (5.1) apply". SeeAlso on test item (R-19).
- **7.1 review**: "consideration of the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of selection and determination activities, and the results of these activities, with regard to fulfilment of specified requirements by an object of conformity assessment".
- **7.2 decision**: "conclusion, based on the results of review, that fulfilment of specified requirements has or has not been demonstrated".
- **7.3 attestation**: "issue of a statement, based on a decision (7.2), that fulfilment of specified requirements (5.1) has been demonstrated". Quoted in the glossary without the internal cross-reference numbers. Status pending a named verification on the OBP.
