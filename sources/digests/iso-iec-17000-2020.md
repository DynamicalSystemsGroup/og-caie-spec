# ISO/IEC 17000:2020(en) Conformity assessment — Vocabulary — digest

Source: `src:iso-iec-17000-2020`, cite-only (Online Browsing Platform, clause 3
publicly browsable). Read on the OBP by Claude on 2026-09-06 for the party terms; the party terms ticked by Z on sheet 03; the later clauses await sheet 04.

- **4.2 object of conformity assessment**: "entity to which specified requirements (5.1) apply". SeeAlso on test item (R-19).
- **7.1 review**: "consideration of the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of selection and determination activities, and the results of these activities, with regard to fulfilment of specified requirements by an object of conformity assessment".
- **7.2 decision**: "conclusion, based on the results of review, that fulfilment of specified requirements has or has not been demonstrated".
- **7.3 attestation**: "issue of a statement, based on a decision (7.2), that fulfilment of specified requirements (5.1) has been demonstrated". Quoted in the glossary without the internal cross-reference numbers. Status pending a named verification on the OBP.
- **4.3 first-party conformity assessment activity**: "conformity assessment activity that is performed by the person or organization that provides or that is the object of conformity assessment (4.2)". Note 1: the descriptors "are not to be confused with the legal identification of the relevant parties to a contract".
- **4.4 second-party conformity assessment activity**: "conformity assessment activity that is performed by a person or organization that has a user interest in the object of conformity assessment (4.2)".
- **4.5 third-party conformity assessment activity**: "conformity assessment activity that is performed by a person or organization that is independent of the provider of the object of conformity assessment (4.2) and has no user interest in the object".
- **4.6 conformity assessment body**: "body that performs conformity assessment activities, excluding accreditation (7.7)". Alternative anchor for account executive (C-26).
- **4.13 owner**: "person or organization responsible for the development and maintenance of a conformity assessment system (4.8) or conformity assessment scheme (4.9)". Not adopted: the owner of a scheme, not of the object.
- **5.4 independence**: "freedom of a person or organization from the control or authority of another person or organization". Behind epo:independentOfAccountable.
- **4.1 conformity assessment, Note 3**: "Conformity assessment is explained in Annex A as a series of functions. Activities contributing to any of these functions can be described as conformity assessment activities." The frame for the seven steps (R-31).
- **4.10 access**: "opportunity for an applicant to obtain a conformity assessment (4.1) service from a body under a conformity assessment scheme (4.9)". SeeAlso on C2 propose (R-32).
- **5.1 specified requirement**: "need or expectation that is stated". Matches the declare-requirements step.
- **6.2 testing**: "determination of one or more characteristics of an object of conformity assessment (4.2), according to a procedure (5.2)". Matches the execute step.
- **7.3 attestation, Note 1**: "The resulting statement, referred to in this document as a “statement of conformity”, is intended to convey the assurance that the specified requirements have been fulfilled." SeeAlso on C5 deliver (R-32).
- **Annex A, Principles of conformity assessment (informative; headings public, body not)**: A.1 Functional approach, A.2 Selection, A.3 Determination, A.4 Review, decision and attestation, A.5 Need for surveillance. Cited by heading only.
- **4.9 conformity assessment scheme**: "set of rules and procedures (5.2) that describes the objects of conformity assessment (4.2), identifies the specified requirements (5.1) and provides the methodology for performing conformity assessment (4.1)". The contract-level frame: objects, requirements, method (R-32).
- **9.6 acceptance**: "use of a conformity assessment result provided by another person or organization". Matches the accept step.
- **8.1 surveillance**: "systematic iteration of conformity assessment activities as a basis for maintaining the validity of the statement of conformity". What a repeat evaluation after a vendor's change is called; observed in practice, outside the contracting lifecycle (R-35).
