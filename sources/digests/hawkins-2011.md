# Hawkins, Kelly, Knight, Graydon (2011), A New Approach to Creating Clear Safety Arguments — digest

Source: `src:hawkins-2011`, author copy (University of York), held locally.
PDF pages of the author copy.

- p. 6: "These assertions relate to the sufficiency and appropriateness of the inferences declared in the argument, the context and assumptions used and the evidence cited."
- p. 6: three kinds of assurance claim point: asserted inference (ACP1), asserted context (ACP2), asserted solution (ACP3).
- p. 7, §3.2 Asserted context: "it is being asserted that the context is appropriate for the argument elements to which it applies"
- p. 10, §3.3 Asserted solution: "it is being asserted that the evidence put forward is sufficient to support the claim"

Ruling R-08: this is the primary source for the pairing appropriateness (of context) and sufficiency (of evidence); "adequacy" is retired.
