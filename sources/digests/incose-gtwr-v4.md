# INCOSE Guide to Writing Requirements v4, Summary Sheet — digest

Source: `src:incose-gtwr-v4` (INCOSE-TP-2010-006-04, June 2023), held locally,

- **C7 Verifiable**: "The need statement is structured and worded such that its realization can be validated to the approving authority's satisfaction. The requirement statement is structured and worded such that its realization can be verified to the approving authority's satisfaction."
- **C14 Able to be validated** and **R4 Defined Terms** ("Define all terms used within the need statement and requirement statement within an associated glossary and/or data dictionary") are the two other characteristics this specification leans on.
